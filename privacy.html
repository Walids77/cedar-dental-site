Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

Cedar Dental Clinic is a demonstration clinic. This booking assistant is a portfolio project.

What we collect

When you book through our booking assistant we collect your full name, phone number, email address, the day and time of your appointment, and the reason for your visit with a short note in your own words.

When you write to us on WhatsApp we also receive your WhatsApp phone number, your messages, and your replies to our confirmation buttons.

How we use it

  • To answer your questions and to book, move or cancel your appointment.
  • To place the appointment in the clinic's Google Calendar and send you a calendar invite by email.
  • On WhatsApp, your WhatsApp number is used as your phone number, so the assistant can only book, move or cancel appointments made with your own number.

We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

WhatsApp and the AI assistant

Your messages are answered by an AI assistant. To do this, your data passes through these service providers, which process it only to run the service:

  • Meta (WhatsApp) delivers the messages between you and the clinic.
  • Microsoft Azure runs the assistant. It keeps the conversation with your WhatsApp number so the assistant remembers what you said earlier, and it keeps small technical records: your WhatsApp number, message IDs (to avoid answering the same message twice) and a message count (to block spam).
  • Google holds the clinic calendar and sends the calendar invite.

Nothing is booked, moved or cancelled until you confirm it yourself. The assistant gives general clinic information only and is not medical advice.

Google user data

The booking app accesses only the clinic's own Google Calendar, to read free times and to create, change or delete appointments. It does not access any patient's Google account. The app's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Keeping and deleting your data

Appointment details are kept in the clinic calendar. WhatsApp conversations and the technical records above are kept in Microsoft Azure while the service runs. To ask us to delete any of your data, email cedardental.demo@gmail.com with your WhatsApp or phone number.
