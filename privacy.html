Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

What we collect

When you book through our booking assistant we collect your full name, phone number, email address, and the day and time of your appointment.

How we use it

  • To book, move or cancel your appointment.
  • To place the appointment in the clinic's Google Calendar and send you a calendar invite by email.

We do not sell your data, use it for advertising, or share it with anyone else.

Google user data

The booking app accesses only the clinic's own Google Calendar, to read free times and to create, change or delete appointments. It does not access any patient's Google account. The app's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Keeping and deleting your data

Appointment details are kept in the clinic calendar. To ask us to delete them, email cedardental.demo@gmail.com.
