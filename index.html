Booking

How booking works

Message us

Tell the assistant the day you want. It checks the clinic diary and shows the free times.

Confirm

Give your full name, phone and email, then say yes. Nothing is booked until you confirm.

Get your invite

The visit goes into our calendar and you receive a calendar invite by email.

The assistant handles bookings and clinic information only. It never gives medical advice. Please cancel at least 24 hours before your visit.
